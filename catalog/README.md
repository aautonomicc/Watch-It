# Public-domain starter library

**[⬇ Download `Public Domain.watch-list`](https://github.com/aautonomicc/Watch-It/raw/main/catalog/Public%20Domain.watch-list)** (1.6 MB)

A ready-made W@tch library of **40 verified-public-domain titles** —
the set the app itself seeded up to alpha.50, before the built-in
catalog was trimmed to *Night of the Living Dead* only. The uploads
live permanently on the Autonomi network; this bundle is the set of
keys (datamaps) plus artwork and descriptions. Import it and you get
the populated poster wall shown in the [README screenshot](../README.md).

## What's inside

| List | Contents |
|---|---|
| Movies | 8 films: *The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari* (1920), *Nanook of the North* (1922), *Nosferatu* (1922), *Battleship Potemkin* (1925), *The General* (1926), *His Girl Friday* (1940), *Charade* (1963), and *Night of the Living Dead* (1968) in two versions (480p and 1080p — one card, version picker) |
| TV Shows | *Petticoat Junction* season 1 (episodes 1–15) and *One Step Beyond* (17 episodes across seasons 1–2) |

41 datamaps in total, with posters and descriptions bundled in — the
library displays fully offline, no TMDB key needed. It's a standard
`.watch-list` bundle ([spec](../docs/BUNDLE-FORMAT.md)).

## How to import

1. Download the file (link above) to your device.
2. Open W@tch, tap the **☰ menu** (top right) and choose **Media**.
3. Tap **＋ Add to library** in the top bar and pick
   `Public Domain.watch-list` from where you saved it.
   (Android sometimes renames downloads to `.bin` — that's fine, the
   importer looks at the content, not the extension.)
4. The import dialog shows the bundle's two lists, **Movies** and
   **TV Shows**. Confirm the import.
   - If your library already has lists with those names (fresh installs
     seed a "Movies" list), choose **merge** when asked — entries you
     already have are detected by address and skipped, nothing is
     duplicated.

Posters and descriptions appear immediately. Playing or downloading a
title streams it from the live Autonomi network, so you need a network
connection for that part — but no account, key, or server.

## Public-domain note

These titles were verified public domain **in the United States**
(published without a copyright notice, or copyright not renewed).
Copyright terms differ elsewhere — in much of Europe protection runs
for 70 years after the death of the last author — so outside the US,
check your local rules.

Removed from this bundle on 2026-08-11 — if you imported an earlier
copy, consider deleting these entries from your library:

- *The Lady Vanishes* (1938): although its US copyright was not
  renewed, it was restored by the 1994 URAA (a Notice of Intent to
  Enforce was filed in 1998), so it is not in the US public domain
  after all.
- *The Hunchback of Notre Dame* (1939): the non-renewal claim rests on
  a single Catalog of Copyright Entries search and could not be
  independently confirmed, so it was removed out of caution.
- *Petticoat Junction* S01E16–E21: these episodes first aired in 1964,
  so the season's stated 1963 non-renewal basis cannot cover them
  (works from 1964 onward were auto-renewed by the 1992 Renewal Act).
  Episodes 1–15 (1963) remain.
